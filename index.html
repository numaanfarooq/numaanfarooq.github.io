The payment status nobody could explain

Handshake AI, L2 Payments
The problem

A steady stream of payment disputes kept escalating. Users couldn’t tell where their payment actually stood, and support kept answering the same confusion case by case.

The analysis

I went through 2,000+ dispute tickets and categorized the recurring process and communication gaps behind the avoidable escalations, using SQL, BigQuery and structured ticket review.

The root cause

A systemic mismatch: internal payment-review stages didn’t map to the statuses users were shown. The system was working; the story it told users wasn’t.

The fix

I proposed a simplified user-facing status framework with SLA-based improvements, and took it through management review to approval for engineering implementation.
